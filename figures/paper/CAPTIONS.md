# Captions for figures/paper

**P1_pipeline_today_vs_v2** — (a) today's reference system (one listwise flagship call over a fixed 30-candidate shortlist; prices and token counts from the real ledger); it remains the fixed-rung system used for the ladder measurement. (b) the proposed v2 system of `docs/04_method_redesign.md` §3: calibrated retriever scores prune the shortlist, one flagship call returns per-candidate scores fused with the retriever prior, a three-way output derived from an elicited loss ratio, verification under dimension permutation, and correlation clustering. Prices are real token counts × fitted relay rates; quality claims are simulated and conditional on the two gates (`sim/README.md` decision table).

**P2_nested_ladder_v2** — the proposed nested ladder. Right column: the closest rung in today's grid and its real mean prompt length (dev split). Note that today's L5 and L5-P still show the title; only L6 removes it, and L2-T is a subset-with-substitution (longer than L1).

**P3_unmixing_schematic** — illustrative: the observed ladder is a mixture of a flat memorised stratum and a falling evidence-only stratum; stratifying by the evidence-free forced-choice probe recovers the true evidence drop. Shapes are schematic, not measured.
